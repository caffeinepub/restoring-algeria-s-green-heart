# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Build an awareness website for Algeria's reforestation efforts, showcasing the Green Dam project, native forest species, and volunteer opportunities.

**Planned changes:**
- Create hero section with "Restoring Algeria's Green Heart" headline and Atlas Mountains background video
- Build Crisis section explaining deforestation causes (wildfires in Kabylie/El Tarf 2021-2024, droughts exceeding 45°C, illegal logging, soil erosion) with "30,000+ hectares annually" statistic
- Create Solution section for Green Dam (1,500 km x 20 km green belt from 1970s, revitalized as biological shield)
- Build interactive volunteer portal with sign-up forms for planting events and digital tree adoption feature
- Create educational blog with articles "How to plant in arid soil" and "The Importance of Mycorrhizae in Algerian Reforestation"
- Add species showcase for Aleppo Pine (drought-resilient backbone), Atlas Cedar (endangered, Chréa/Theniet El Had parks), and Cork Oak (cork industry, biodiversity)
- Add National Reforestation Plan section (state-led, billions of trees over next decade)
- Add Urban Greening section (green ratio laws for heat islands in Algiers/Oran)
- Add Executive Summary (Algeria as Africa's largest country, 4.1M hectares forest cover, dual mission)
- Apply Deep Forest Green primary color and Desert Sand accent color throughout

**User-visible outcome:** Users can explore Algeria's reforestation crisis and solutions, learn about native species, read educational content, and sign up for volunteer events or adopt trees digitally.
